Sign-in and roles

One account, several roles. People sign in once, with a work email over OAuth, which matches them to their company’s account.

They land in the area of their primary role. The nav gets an area switcher if they hold more than one; for example, an admin is usually also an employee.

The quick-actions launcher only shows actions the current role can use: Create Agent, Tool, Skill and MCP Server for admins, and Start Conversation for clients and employees.

Open questions

  1. Are “employees” the clients’ staff, who work alongside the services (this draft’s assumption), or our own staff, or both?
  2. Does the Product screen’s audience (the product’s PM and supporting-function managers) belong in Admin, as drafted, or in a separate internal area apart from the people who build agents?
  3. For clients, what separates a “service” from a “product page”? The draft treats services as what’s running for the organization, and product pages as offerings it can adopt.
  4. Should the route moves (for example, the landing to / and the Dashboard to /app) happen now, while everything is mock data, or together with auth?
